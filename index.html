🏘️ Our mission

What is ApnaMohalla?

Help is closer than you think.

In India, help usually travels through chaotic WhatsApp forwards and word of mouth. ApnaMohalla brings it all into one trusted, hyperlocal place — so the person who needs help and the neighbour who can give it actually find each other, fast.

Whether it's an urgent blood requirement, a lost pet, a trusted plumber, or an emergency, your mohalla has the answer. We just make it easy to reach.

    Why neighbours trust us

  • 💚 Built for India, not adapted from abroad
  • 🤝 Hyperlocal — help from your own area
  • ⚡ WhatsApp-first — no app download needed
  • 🔒 Verified people, not anonymous strangers
